Data collection and privacy

We only store data that is required to run the game. This data is not shared with anyone, and is only used to make the game work!

What information is stored?

  • Player statistics: how many games you've played, won, lost etc.
  • Match history: the outcome of the games you've played.
  • User name: the name you chose for your profile.
  • Email address: providing you have opted to link your email address with your profile in order to log in across devices.
  • Profile picture: providing you opted to upload one.

No other personal information is accessed or stored.

Data deletion

If you'd like to delete your account, please send an email to removeme@dontlookdown.ca.

Please include your Player ID, which you can find at the bottom of the Edit Profile screen.
