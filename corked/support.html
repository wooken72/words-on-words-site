Support

Need a hand? Email accounts@dontlookdown.ca and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two. It helps to include your player ID or username (shown in Settings) and what phone you're using.

How do I release a bottle?

Head to a river or the coast. When you're close enough to water, tap the release button, write your message (add a photo if you like) and let it go. The bottle then drifts on real ocean currents, river flow and wind.

How do I find bottles?

Bottles drifting near you show on the map. Tap the binoculars to fly to the nearest one. When it's inside the blue ring around you, tap it to collect it, read its messages and add your own before it drifts on. Everything you've collected or released is kept in your Bottle Library.

A bottle is too far away to reach

If it's within the dashed ring, you can freeze it in place for 6 hours while you go to collect it. Look for the snowflake beside it on the map.

The app can't find my location

Corked needs your location to release and find bottles. Open the iPhone's Settings → Corked → Location and choose While Using the App, with Precise Location turned on.

I'm not getting notifications

Check Settings → Notifications → Corked on your iPhone, then the Notifications section of the app's Settings, where you can choose which ones you get.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Link your account with Sign in with Apple in the app's Settings, then sign in the same way on your new phone.

Someone wrote something they shouldn't have

Tap Report under the message and we'll review it within 24 hours. Tap Block to stop seeing that player's messages, photos and bottles straight away. See the Terms of Use for what's allowed.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. The Privacy Policy explains what's removed.
