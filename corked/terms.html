Terms of Use

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: Corked is a shared world, and every message you write is read by real people. Be kind. There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive players: we remove messages and photos that break these rules, and ban players who post them. Report anything that shouldn't be there, and block anyone you don't want to hear from.

These terms apply to the Corked: Message in a Bottle app (“Corked”, “we”, “us”). By tapping I agree in the app, or by using Corked, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use Corked.

Who can play

You must be at least 13 years old to use Corked. If you're under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission.

What you can't post

Bottle names, messages, usernames and photos are seen by other players. Don't post anything that is:

  • bullying, harassing, threatening or hateful, including attacks on anyone for their race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, sexual orientation or age;
  • sexual, pornographic or sexually suggestive, or that involves minors in any inappropriate way;
  • violent, gory or encouraging self-harm, or encouraging anything illegal or dangerous;
  • personal information, yours or anyone else's, such as addresses, phone numbers, email addresses or social media handles;
  • spam, advertising or scams;
  • impersonating someone else, including us;
  • someone else's work or photo that you don't have the right to share.

Messages are checked automatically when you write them, and some will be refused. That check isn't perfect, so these rules apply whether or not a message gets through.

Reporting and blocking

Tap Report under any message to tell us about it. Tap Block to hide a player's messages, photos and bottles from you straight away. Our team is told about every report and block, and we review reports within 24 hours. Players aren't told who reported or blocked them.

What we do about it

We can remove any message, photo, bottle name or username that breaks these rules, and suspend or permanently ban players who post them, without warning. We may do the same to protect other players or keep the game working. If you're banned, you can't release bottles, collect them or write messages.

Playing safely

Corked is played in the real world, near water. Watch where you're going, stay away from unsafe water, banks and cliffs, respect private property and local rules, and never put yourself or others at risk to reach a bottle. You play at your own risk.

Your content

You keep the rights to what you write and the photos you take. By putting them in a bottle, you let us store them and show them to other players as part of the game, for as long as the bottle is in the game. Bottles keep drifting after you stop playing; see the Privacy Policy for what happens when you delete your account.

The game

Corked is provided as it is. Bottles drift on simulated currents, and we can change, reset or remove bottles, features or the game itself. We aren't responsible for anything other players write. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for any loss or harm from using Corked.

Changes

If we change these terms, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, the app will ask you to agree again.
