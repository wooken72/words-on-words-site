How it works

Corked is a message-in-a-bottle game played on a real map of the world. Your bottle is carried by real ocean currents, river flow and wind.

Release

Head to a river, lake or beach near you. Name your bottle, write a message, add a photo if you like, and let it go.

Drift

Your bottle is on its own now. It might wash up a few towns away next week, or cross an ocean over the months ahead.

Find

When someone's bottle drifts within reach of you, collect it, read every message inside, add your own and send it on.

A sea chart of messages

Every bottle in the world drifts on the same shared map, so the one bobbing past your coast could have come from anywhere.

What's in the bottle

Add your words

Finding a bottle doesn't end its journey. Add your message and it keeps drifting, growing into a chain of notes from people and places along the way.

Send a snapshot

Tuck a photo in with your message. Whoever finds your bottle sees it develop like an old Polaroid.

Hear back

Get word whenever someone finds a bottle you released or wrote in, with where it washed up and how far it travelled.

Follow every journey

Watch your bottles' trails across the globe, keep the ones you've found in your Bottle Library, and favourite any bottle to see where it goes next.

Make it yours

Name your bottle and choose from a collection of illustrated bottles, from classic green glass to a ship in a bottle.

A friendly sea

Exact release spots are never shown to other players, only the nearest town. Messages are checked, and anything unkind can be reported.

Collect them all

Dozens of medals for releasing, finding and writing, for bottles that travel the furthest, and for special moments like being first to find a bottle.

Find some water

Grab a message, head for the shore, and see where the tide takes it.
