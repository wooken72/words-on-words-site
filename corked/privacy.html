Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: Corked needs your location to let you release and find bottles near you. The messages and photos you put in bottles are meant to be read by other players. Exact places you release bottles are never shown to anyone. We don't sell your information, show ads or track you across other apps. You can delete your account at any time from the app's Settings.

This policy explains what information Corked: Message in a Bottle (“Corked”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use the Corked app and this website, why, and what choices you have.

What we collect

Your player account. When you first open the app, we create an account for you automatically. It has a random player ID (such as K7Q2MX) and the country you were in when it was created; other players can see both. You can also choose a username, which other players see in place of your player ID; you can change it at any time in Settings. We also keep an identifier for your device and a sign-in token, stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, so the app can sign you back in.

Sign in with Apple (optional). If you link your account with Sign in with Apple so you can keep your progress on a new phone, Apple shares an identifier for you with our account provider (see below). If Apple shares an email address, it may be a private relay address. We don't use it to contact you.

Your location. While you're using the app, it uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map and which water and bottles are within reach. Your location is sent to our server when you release a bottle, collect one or add a message, so we can check you're close enough. We store where each bottle was released and where bottles were collected. The app doesn't use your location in the background.

Messages, bottle names and photos. The names you give bottles, the messages you write and any photos you add are stored on our servers and shared with every player who collects that bottle. A bottle's name is visible to everyone. Please don't include personal details such as your address or phone number; messages containing contact details are rejected.

Photos. Photos are only accessed when you choose to take one or pick one from your library, and only the photo you choose. Before uploading, the app crops it and saves it as a new image, which removes hidden information such as where and when it was taken.

Your play. We keep the bottles you've released, collected and favourited, the badges you've earned and the news in your Inbox.

Reports and blocked players. If you report a message, we keep the report and any reason you give so we can review it. If you block a player, we keep a list of the players you've blocked so we can hide their messages, photos and bottles from you. When you report a message or block a player, our team is sent an email with both players' IDs and names, and the message and photo involved, so we can check whether anything needs to be done.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we keep a token from Apple that lets us send them to your phone.

Technical information. Our servers don't keep logs of your requests. Like any internet service, our network provider processes your IP address to deliver the app's data and protect against abuse. The game engine our app is built with (Unity) may send basic, anonymous hardware information, such as your phone model and iOS version, to Unity Technologies.

What other players can see

  • Your username (or, if you haven't chosen one, your player ID) and your country's flag, next to bottles you release, bottles you find and messages you write.
  • The messages and photos in a bottle, but only once they've collected it.
  • The town nearest where a bottle was released, and its trail across the map. The exact spot is never shown: release points appear only as an area of about 2 km, and trails fade out as they get near it.

Other players aren't told when you report their message or block them.

How we use it

  • To run the game: drifting bottles, collecting, messages, badges and news.
  • To keep the game safe: checking messages, reviewing reports and blocks, hiding blocked players from those who blocked them, and removing messages or banning players who break the rules.
  • To keep the service working and fix problems.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who helps us

  • brainCloud (bitHeads Inc., Canada) runs player accounts, Sign in with Apple, photo storage and notifications for us.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic between the app and our servers.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, notifications and the App Store.
  • Google (Gmail) sends the emails about reports and blocks to our team.

They process information only to provide these services to us. The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data served by our own servers. If you ask for directions to water or a bottle, the app opens a map app on your phone with that destination, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. Bottles keep drifting in the shared world, so released bottles and their messages stay in the game after you've finished playing. Database backups are kept for seven days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account. This deletes your player account, your brainCloud profile, your photos and your list of blocked players (and you're removed from other players' lists). Bottles you released and messages you wrote stay in the world for other players, but they're no longer linked to you, and their photos are removed. It may take up to seven days for your information to leave our backups.

Messages and photos that break the rules may be removed by us at any time.

Your choices

  • You can block a player from any of their messages, and unblock them under Settings → Blocked players.
  • You can turn off location, camera, photo or notification access at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Without location, you can still explore the map, but you can't release or collect bottles.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us below.

Children

Corked isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, sign-in tokens are stored scrambled, and admin tools are only available from our private network. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app's Inbox.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: accounts@dontlookdown.ca
